# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Replift to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, whatever their age, body size, ability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, religion or background.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Publishing other people's private information
- Trolling, or anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break this code, and may ban people who do. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" option or contact the maintainer through the repository owner's profile. Reports are handled privately.
